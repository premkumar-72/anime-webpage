POPULAR

Iconic & Long-Running Franchises

These anime have achieved massive global recognition and have been popular for decades:

Dragon Ball (including Dragon Ball Z): A foundational shonen anime following the martial artist Goku and his friends as they defend Earth from increasingly powerful threats. It is often cited as the "grandfather" of modern shonen anime.

One Piece: The story of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew searching for the ultimate treasure, the "One Piece". It is currently the best-selling manga series by a single author and is known for its extensive world-building and emotional depth.

Naruto (including Naruto: Shippuden): Follows a young, ostracized ninja who seeks recognition and dreams of becoming the leader of his village, the Hokage.

Attack on Titan: A dark fantasy series where humanity fights for survival against giant humanoid creatures called Titans. The series concluded in 2023.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba: Follows Tanjiro Kamado, who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered and his sister is turned into a demon.
